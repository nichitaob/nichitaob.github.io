Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Lead Finder is a private tool used only by its owner, Nichita (contact: nichitaob@gmail.com). It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

It uses the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly) on the owner's own account only.

It searches only for notification emails from Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Alerts and downloads only those. Other emails are not downloaded.

It never sends, deletes or modifies emails.

How the data is used

The notifications are filtered on the owner's computer to find public requests for marketing services.

Names and contact details are removed or hidden before any further processing.

A short, cleaned text may be analysed by an AI service (Anthropic Claude) to classify the request.

A short alert is sent to the owner's own private Telegram bot.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in an encrypted container on the owner's computer.

Email texts are deleted after 3 days. Alerts are deleted after at most 47 hours.

The access token is kept in the Windows Credential Manager and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lead Finder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

If you appear in a notification and want your data removed, or want to object, write to nichitaob@gmail.com.
