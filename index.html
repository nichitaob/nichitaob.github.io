Lead Finder

Lead Finder is a private tool used only by its owner, Nichita, an independent marketing freelancer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It runs on the owner's own computer. With read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account, it reads notification emails from Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Alerts, so that the owner can see requests for marketing services and decide himself whether to reply.

The tool never sends, deletes or changes emails.
